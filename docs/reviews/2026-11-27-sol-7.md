# Multi-select re-review of `745be4b`

Read-only review. **0 critical, 2 important, 1 minor finding.**

### Important

1. **A copy from a new source app still drops selected rows and cancels pending deletion.** A history refresh initially fetches through the last visible row. When the source-app list changes, `HistoryView` rebuilds an equivalent filter object. `useHistory` treats that as a filter change and starts a new 60-row request. I selected 60 clips and opened the delete confirmation, then added one clip from a new source app. The selection fell to 59 and the confirmation disappeared. I also reproduced selection loss in a 6,000-clip history after loading 5,040 rows. Any refresh at that depth drops back to 5,000 rows, even when the selected clip still matches. [HistoryView.tsx:64](../../src/renderer/src/features/history/HistoryView.tsx:64), [useHistory.ts:212](../../src/renderer/src/features/history/useHistory.ts:212)
2. **Composed-text suppression can silently discard genuine copies indefinitely.** After a batch copy, the content hash can suppress an identical copy from another app even when the native clipboard sequence advances and the app’s write marker is absent. I reproduced this 24 hours after the batch copy and again after restoring different text in between. The marker check returns early on that restore, leaving the old hash active. The original missing-marker and expired-token cases are covered, but the fallback should use the clipboard version where available. [capture-service.ts:342](../../src/main/clipboard/capture-service.ts:342), [capture-service.ts:383](../../src/main/clipboard/capture-service.ts:383)
