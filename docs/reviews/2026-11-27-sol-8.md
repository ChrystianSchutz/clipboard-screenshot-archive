## Important

1. **A genuine copy can inherit the composed write’s clipboard version.** `writeComposedText` reads the version after the asynchronous write resolves. If another app copies the same text after the clipboard write commits but before that read, the guard saves the other app’s version and suppresses its copy. I reproduced this with a versioned clipboard fake. [capture-service.ts:238](../../src/main/clipboard/capture-service.ts:238)
2. **The portable fallback drops detectable copies for up to two minutes.** After the poller observed the app’s marked write, I simulated another app copying the same text without the marker. The poller reported the change, but capture returned `self-write` because the text matched the live guard. The timeout fixes indefinite suppression and still leaves a two-minute window for genuine copies to disappear. [capture-service.ts:411](../../src/main/clipboard/capture-service.ts:411), [watchers.ts:216](../../src/main/clipboard/watchers.ts:216)
3. **Deep history refreshes can keep restarting and leave the view stale.** Every change restarts the full reload and discards any older refresh still in progress. With 5,040 rows loaded, I reproduced six changes that each restarted pagination from the top. The newest rows appeared only after the changes stopped. At 50,000 loaded clips, one refresh takes at least 100 sequential queries, or about 391 with full-length previews. [useHistory.ts:98](../../src/renderer/src/features/history/useHistory.ts:98), [useHistory.ts:177](../../src/renderer/src/features/history/useHistory.ts:177), [useHistory.ts:227](../../src/renderer/src/features/history/useHistory.ts:227)

## Minor

- **A failed restore can cause the app to capture its own composed text.** Starting a restore clears the guard before the clipboard changes. I reproduced a restore that failed before writing, followed by a change report whose marker read returned `null`. Capture recorded the still-current composed text. [capture-service.ts:256](../../src/main/clipboard/capture-service.ts:256)
