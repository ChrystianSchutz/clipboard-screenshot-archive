## Important

1. **A successful page load can hide a failed refresh and drop a selected clip.** Re-copy a selected clip after a refresh has read its first page. The clip moves above the cursor and is temporarily retained in `missing`. If the trailing refresh fails and a queued Load more succeeds, the page load clears both `missing` and the error even though it only fetched older rows. The clip disappears from the selection and stays out of the list until another history change. [useHistory.ts:240](../../src/renderer/src/features/history/useHistory.ts:240), [useHistory.ts:259](../../src/renderer/src/features/history/useHistory.ts:259)
2. **Ctrl+A can add a hidden clip to a batch delete.** With a re-copied clip temporarily absent from History, Ctrl+A includes its ID alongside the rendered rows. I reproduced a batch delete sending four IDs while the list displayed three rows. ArrowDown also landed on the hidden clip and set `aria-activedescendant` to an element that did not exist. [HistoryView.tsx:80](../../src/renderer/src/features/history/HistoryView.tsx:80), [HistoryView.tsx:250](../../src/renderer/src/features/history/HistoryView.tsx:250), [HistoryView.tsx:289](../../src/renderer/src/features/history/HistoryView.tsx:289)
