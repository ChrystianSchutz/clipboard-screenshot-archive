Features

Never lose a copy again

You copy a link, an error message and a bit of code. Two days later you need one of them back. Press Ctrl+Alt+V, type a few letters, press Enter. Done.

Clipboard history

Know where every copy came from

Every copy is saved with the app it came from. Copy from a browser, and you get the web page too.

  • See the app and website next to each copy
  • Copy the same thing twice? It moves to the top. No duplicates.
  • Pin what you want to keep
  • Press Enter to put a copy back on your clipboard

The app and website are shown on Windows. Everything else works on macOS and Linux too.

Screenshot text

Search the text inside your screenshots

Take a screenshot of an error. It lands in your history, and its text is read right away, on your computer.

  • Search a word, and the screenshot shows up
  • Select a line with your mouse and copy it
  • Or copy all of the text in one click
  • Works offline, in English and Polish

Optional: let it describe the picture

A small model (22 MB) runs on your computer. It describes the screenshot and lists what it sees. It is off until you turn it on.

  • Off (default)
  • On demand
  • Automatic

On macOS, turn on screenshot import in Settings.

Privacy

Your clipboard stays on your computer

Your clipboard sees passwords and private messages. So nothing leaves your computer. No account. No cloud. No tracking.

  • Saved on your computer

    Your history is one file on your computer. Nothing is uploaded.

  • Passwords are skipped

    When an app marks a copy as private, like a password, it is never saved.

  • Block any app

    Choose apps that are never recorded. KeePass, KeePassXC, 1Password and Bitwarden are blocked from the start.

  • Pause any time

    One click in the tray or in Settings stops recording.

  • Secrets are flagged

    Keys and tokens get a Secret tag, so you can find and delete them fast.

  • Works offline

    Screenshot text is read on your computer. The only download is the optional image model, and only when you ask for it.

Private-copy detection and app blocking work on Windows.

Built right

Secure by default, tested end to end

For developers: the app follows Electron's security checklist, and the tests run the real app without ever touching your desktop.

  • Context isolation and sandbox

    Every window is sandboxed, with no Node.js access.

  • Typed, validated IPC

    One typed contract. Every call is checked: who sent it, and what is in it.

  • Strict CSP

    No inline scripts, no eval, nothing loaded from outside.

  • No remote content

    The whole UI ships inside the app and loads from its own protocol.

  • Locked navigation

    No pop-up windows and no navigation away from the app.

  • Heavy work isolated

    Text recognition runs in its own process. Its results are checked before saving.

  • Fuses and ASAR integrity

    Debug switches are off, and a tampered app package will not start.

  • Permissions denied

    Camera, mic and every other permission request is refused.

A test suite that stays out of your way

The end-to-end tests drive the real app in a hidden sandbox.

  • Windows render but are never shown
  • A fake clipboard, so yours is never touched
  • A fresh profile for every test

Every screenshot on this page comes from that sandbox.

Install

Up and running in a minute

Grab the installer for your system from GitHub Releases. Every file comes with a SHA-256 checksum.

  • Windows

    .exe installer

    The builds are not code-signed yet, so SmartScreen says "Windows protected your PC". Click More info, then Run anyway.

  • macOS

    .dmg disk image

    Gatekeeper refuses an unsigned app the first time. Right-click it in Applications, choose Open, then confirm.

  • Linux

    .AppImage

    Make it executable, then run it:

    chmod +x *.AppImage

Rather build it yourself? With Node 24+: npm ci, then npm run dev. The app starts in the tray.

Keyboard

Hands stay on the keyboard

On macOS, use Cmd in place of Ctrl for the two global shortcuts. Both can be re-recorded in Settings.

Anywhere and in the window
Ctrl+Alt+V Open or hide the history, from any app
Ctrl+Alt+D Compare: press after copying A, again after B
Ctrl+1–4 History, Screenshots, Stats, Settings
Ctrl+R Switch to regular-expression search
Esc Clear the mark, then the search, then hide
On the selected copy
Enter Put it back on your clipboard
Ctrl+P Pin or unpin
Ctrl+D Mark it, then compare it with the next one
Del Delete
Shift+F10 Open the right-click menu
